# SCI-FI NOODLES design brief

Design read: Sci-fi fans and curious makers enter an independent studio, not a creator portfolio, and find something to watch in one scroll.

Concept spine: The site is a kitchen in orbit. A black bowl of cosmic noodles is the studio. Scroll pulls one strand, and each strand is a different shelf of the channel.

Delivery tier: cinema

Locked palette: User brand, explicit override of the default bans. Deep space navy #090E1A and near black #05070C dominate. Electric cyan #00E8FF is the only interface accent. Violet #9B5CFF and magenta #FF4FD8 stay inside imagery. Cool light #C7D5FF and soft white #F3F6FA carry type. Muted #8E9AAD. Panel #101827. Defense: these hexes are the published SCI-FI NOODLES brand kit.

Locked type: Outfit for display and body (geometric tech sans, one family). IBM Plex Mono for runtimes, dates, and rights notes. No serif.

Animation mode: animated-website

Journey shape: single-shot

Journey: One ~15s push-in on the cosmic bowl. Chapters read as the hero plus the shelves that follow the same move.
1. Studio. Wide bowl in dark space. Headline: SCI-FI NOODLES. Watch.
2. Originals. The lifted strand becomes a world. T.I.R.A. // RIFT and The Stranger.
3. Classics. The strand passes a reimagined public-domain slate.
4. Atmospheres. Macro along the glow: docs, ambience, shorts.
5. Kitchen. The strand settles. The Noodle Drop.

Camera architecture: single continuous push-in, center-safe, no cuts. Not multi-leg.

Seam direction: none. One clip.

Mobile framing: subject stays center-safe. Same film, lighter 720 encode. Copy sits on a dark scrim at the bottom.

Cost shape: one 16:9 storyboard plus one film. Supporting stills for shelves. No second hero film.

Delivery budget: desktop clip ≤32 MiB, mobile clip ≤16 MiB.

World grammar: Photoreal cinematic science fiction. Deep space navy ground, low-detail darkness, cyan filament light, restrained violet and magenta only inside the noodles. Locked exposure. Slow steady motion. No on-screen text, logos, or watermarks in generated frames.

Section plan:
1. Journey film, full-viewport sticky stage, left copy.
2. Featured premiere, asymmetric split, poster left.
3. Choose your noodle, horizontal shelf rail.
4. Original universes, two unequal panels.
5. Public domain, divided list.
6. Latest slate, compact rows.
7. The Lab, split with image right.
8. Join the kitchen, full-width transmission band.
9. Footer, link columns.

Eyebrow budget: 3. Used only on the journey kicker, the premiere series label, and the join band.

Asset plan: storyboard, one scrub film plus posters, logo from the supplied mark, stills for T.I.R.A., The Stranger, a classic, an atmosphere, a doc, and the lab. No stock.

CTA inventory:
- WatchMark: cyan keyline that fills on hover. Label: Watch.
- ExploreOrbit: text with an orbital underline. Label: Explore.
- EnterBracket: corner-bracket link. Label: Enter.
- JoinTransmit: field plus a send key. Label: Join.
- SignalLink: mono metadata link for YouTube, press, and legal. Not a button.
